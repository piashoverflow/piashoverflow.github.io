Four Pillars of First-Principles STEM Pedagogy

How I transform complex mechanical and mathematical theories into intuitive, indelible mental models.

Intuition Before Formulation

Equations are summaries of physical reality, not starting points. Every concept begins with physical phenomena, tactile thought experiments, and qualitative reasoning before introducing mathematical machinery.

Tabletop Demonstrations

Developed the prominent Golpe Golpe Physics (Physics through Stories) video curriculum, using practical physical props to unveil Archimedes' buoyancy, Lenz's law induction, and gyro precession in action.

Non-Routine Problem Design

Authored multi-concept challenge problems for the elite "Tukhor" batch, training Bangladesh's top prospective engineering students to synthesize mechanics, thermodynamics, and electromagnetism into unified solutions.

Curriculum & Video Production

"Golpe Golpe Physics" Video Curriculum

Created an engaging conceptual physics series designed to eliminate the fear of physics among high school and college students across Bangladesh.

  • Fluid Dynamics: Demonstrating Bernoulli's principle and boundary layer separation using air blowers and venturi tubes.
  • Archimedes & Buoyancy: Revealing buoyant force equilibria and hydrostatic paradoxes via custom tabletop water manometers.
  • Electromagnetic Induction: Visualizing eddy current damping and Faraday-Lenz magnetic braking using neodymium magnets and copper tubes.
  • Rotational Mechanics: Experiencing angular momentum conservation and gyroscopic stability using rotating platforms and bicycle wheels.
